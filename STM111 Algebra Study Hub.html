Polynomials (Week 1)

Degree and types

A polynomial is $p(x)=a_nx^n+\dots+a_1x+a_0$. Its degree is the highest power of $x$. By number of terms: monomial (1), binomial (2), trinomial (3).

Degree of a product = sum of the degrees. Degree of a sum = the highest degree (unless the leading terms cancel).

Operations

  • Add: combine like terms, powers never change.
  • Subtract: Keep–Change–Change, flip every sign in the second bracket.
  • Multiply: distribute every term (FOIL for two binomials), using $a^m\cdot a^n=a^{m+n}$.
$(3x^2+5x-2)+(2x^2-3x+4)=5x^2+2x+2$
$(2x-1)(x^2+3x+4)=2x^3+6x^2+8x-x^2-3x-4=2x^3+5x^2+5x-4$

Factorization (Week 2)

Always start with the GCF

Take the GCF of the coefficients and the lowest power of each shared variable: $12x^3+18x^2=6x^2(2x+3)$.

Special forms

$a^2-b^2=(a-b)(a+b)$
$a^3+b^3=(a+b)(a^2-ab+b^2)$
$a^3-b^3=(a-b)(a^2+ab+b^2)$
$a^2\pm2ab+b^2=(a\pm b)^2$

Trinomials and grouping

  • $x^2+bx+c$: find two numbers with product $c$ and sum $b$.
  • $ax^2+bx+c$: find two numbers with product $ac$ and sum $b$, split the middle term, then group.
  • Four terms: group in pairs and pull out a common binomial.
$2x^2+7x+3=2x^2+6x+x+3=(2x+1)(x+3)$
$x^3-3x^2-4x+12=x^2(x-3)-4(x-3)=(x-3)(x-2)(x+2)$, so the roots are $3,\,2,\,-2$.

Rational expressions and division (Weeks 3–4)

Simplify, add, multiply, divide

  • Simplify: factor top and bottom, cancel common factors only, never common terms.
  • Add/subtract: get a common denominator, then combine the numerators.
  • Multiply: factor, multiply across, simplify.
  • Divide: multiply by the reciprocal of the second fraction.
$\dfrac{7x+35}{x^2+5x}=\dfrac{7(x+5)}{x(x+5)}=\dfrac7x$

Polynomial long division

Dividend = Divisor × Quotient + Remainder

The remainder always has a lower degree than the divisor. If the remainder is $0$, the divisor is a factor.

Example: $(x^3-2x^2+5x-4)\div(x-1)$ gives quotient $x^2-x+4$ and remainder $0$, so $x-1$ is a factor. Dividing by $x+3$ gives remainder $-64$, so $x+3$ is not.

Shortcut check: dividing by $x-a$, the remainder equals $p(a)$.

Complex numbers (Weeks 5–6)

Basics and operations

$z=x+iy$ with $i^2=-1$, $\mathrm{Re}(z)=x$, $\mathrm{Im}(z)=y$. Add and subtract the real and imaginary parts separately.

$(a+bi)(c+di)=(ac-bd)+(ad+bc)i$
$\bar z=x-iy$ and $z\bar z=x^2+y^2$

To divide, multiply top and bottom by the conjugate of the denominator: $\dfrac{10+5i}{1+2i}=\dfrac{(10+5i)(1-2i)}{5}=4-3i$.

Modulus and argument
